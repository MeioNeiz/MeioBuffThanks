# Meio Buff Thanks

## 1.2.0

- Lines per character: tick "Own lines for <name>" in the message editor and that
  character whispers its own lines; your other characters keep the shared list

## 1.1.0

- Thanks come after a random 2 to 6 second wait instead of straight away, so they read like
  a person typing rather than a bot

## 1.0.0

- First release: players who cast a long buff on you get a thank you whisper (your own
  lines, `{name}` and `{spell}` filled in) and/or the /thank emote
